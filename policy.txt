Refund Policy: Customers may request a full refund within 14 days of purchase.
Refunds after 14 days are issued as store credit only, at manager discretion.

Support Plan: The Standard plan includes email support with a 24-hour response time.
24/7 live support is only included in the Premium plan.

Delivery: Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days. Express shipping (paid add-on)
takes 1-2 business days. We do not guarantee same-day delivery under any plan.

Cancellation: Subscriptions can be cancelled anytime and take effect at the end of
the current billing cycle. There is no early-termination fee.
